# ZWSO Methodology v0.2

## Objective

Create one immutable weekly snapshot of the observable global information and cultural environment using public sources only.

The instrument is designed for relative longitudinal comparison, not as a scientific estimate of worldwide public opinion.

## Source families

Each weekly pass should seek evidence across four distinct source families:

1. **News and events**
   - mainstream reporting
   - regional and local reporting
   - multilingual reporting where useful

2. **Public-attitude measurements**
   - polling and surveys
   - consumer confidence
   - search behaviour
   - other direct measurements when available

3. **Culture and behaviour**
   - entertainment
   - sport
   - memes and internet culture
   - creator/social discourse
   - cultural consumption and participation

4. **Slow reality**
   - economic statistics
   - health indicators
   - scientific and climate observations
   - technology adoption
   - markets and other measured conditions

The system must not manufacture balance by selecting positive stories to offset negative news. Instead, disagreement between source families should remain visible.

## Coverage domains

The normal scan covers world affairs and politics, business/economy, technology and AI, entertainment/media, sport, health, cyber/security, climate and significant weather, internet culture/memes, public attitudes toward AI/technology, and notable unexpected cultural developments.

## Snapshot structure

A snapshot should preserve:

- snapshot date and coverage period
- methodology version
- snapshot type
- human-readable synthesis
- category state
- synthetic context indices
- major themes
- emerging themes
- fading themes
- cultural context
- AI-attitude context
- uncertainty and disagreement
- source manifest/provenance
- comparison with the previous snapshot

## Context indices

Indices are model-generated estimates of the observed source environment. They are not population percentages.

Where supported, retain separate measures for media/news mood and broader observed state so journalism's structural negativity is not silently interpreted as the mood of the world.

Scores should be treated as directional and comparative. Confidence and disagreement matter more than false precision.

## Evidence discipline

Keep these distinct:

- **Observed salience**: what is visibly prominent in the source pass.
- **Interpretation**: what those observations may indicate.
- **Uncertainty / disagreement**: where evidence is mixed, regionally divergent or insufficient.

Do not manufacture consensus.

## Historical backfill

Retrospective snapshots are allowed for bootstrapping a time series, but must be labelled `retrospective_backfill`.

They are not equivalent to contemporaneous records because historical search, indexing and knowledge of later events can alter what is discoverable or salient.

Backfilled records remain immutable once written.

## Downstream use

ZWSO should be used as a second-pass contextual overlay rather than ambient prior context.

An idea or claim should be evaluated on its own merits before retrieving ZWSO. ZWSO may then inform timing, saturation, resonance, risk, or contextual framing. It should not revise the frozen intrinsic quality assessment merely because a topic is currently salient.

## Change control

Never rewrite historical snapshots when methodology changes. Increment the methodology version and apply the new method prospectively, or to clearly labelled retrospective backfills.
